Joakim Nguyen is an AI Engineer in the Liu Lab, building and deploying AI/ML systems that turn clinical and biomedical data into practical tools for clinicians and researchers. Joakim's background spans computational pathology, clinical NLP, and multi-agent LLM systems. At the UT Austin [AI Health Lab](https://aihealth.ischool.utexas.edu/), Joakim led a pediatric brain tumor project applying vision foundation models to whole-slide histopathology and built a multi-agent LLM pipeline for thematic analysis of clinical interviews. Before that, as a Machine Learning Engineer at NanoMood, Joakim built biosignal ML pipelines for sleep staging from EEG and heart rate variability, and later, as an AI Consultant at [HealthQuest Capital](https://www.healthquestcapital.com/), built an agentic LLM tool on Azure for document triage.

Joakim holds an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence from UT Austin and a B.S. in Cognitive Science (Machine Learning & Neural Computation) from UC San Diego.
